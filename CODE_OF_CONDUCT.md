# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary. The linked text has the details.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient.
- Respect other opinions and experiences.
- Give feedback about the work, not about the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Own your mistakes, say sorry, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the whole community.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing private information of another person without their clear permission.
- Other conduct that is not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Report a problem

Contact the maintainers in private. Use the private contact options on the maintainers' GitHub organization page. Do not report conduct problems in a public issue.

The maintainers will review each report, keep the reporter's identity private, and answer as fast as they can.

## What the maintainers can do

Depending on how serious the problem is: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project spaces.
